Concept of Definite Integral

Exact Area & Accumulated Change

📌 What is a Definite Integral?

A definite integral gives the exact area between a curve and the x-axis over a fixed interval [a, b].

📐 Mathematical Definition

∫ab f(x) dx = limn→∞ Σ f(xᵢ)Δx

📊 Key Interpretations

  • Represents accumulated change
  • Limit of Riemann sums
  • Exact area under the curve

📈 Graphical Meaning

The shaded region shows the definite integral of y = f(x) from x = 0 to x = 2.

✏ Final Insight

Definite integrals transform approximation into exact measurement and form the foundation of integral calculus.
