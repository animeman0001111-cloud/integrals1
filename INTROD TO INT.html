Introduction to Integration

Understanding area, accumulation, and the inverse of differentiation

📌 What is Integration?

Integration is a fundamental concept in calculus that represents the accumulation of quantities, such as area under a curve. It is also the inverse operation of differentiation.

📐 Notation

∫ f(x) dx
where ∫ is the integral sign
f(x) is the integrand
dx indicates integration with respect to x

📊 Geometric Meaning

  • Represents the area under a curve.
  • Measures total accumulation.
  • Used to compute distance, work, and probability.

📈 Graphical Representation

The shaded region shows the area under the curve y = x².
