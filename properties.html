Properties & Techniques of Integration

Rules that simplify and methods that solve integrals

📌 Properties of Integration

  • ∫[f(x) + g(x)]dx = ∫f(x)dx + ∫g(x)dx
  • ∫k·f(x)dx = k∫f(x)dx
  • ∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx = −∫ᵇₐ f(x)dx
  • If f(x) ≥ 0, then area is positive

📐 Fundamental Property

∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx = F(b) − F(a)

This connects differentiation and integration and is known as the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.

🛠 Technique: Substitution

Used when an integral contains a composite function.

∫ f(g(x))·g′(x) dx = ∫ f(u) du

🛠 Technique: Integration by Parts

Used for products of functions.

∫ u dv = u·v − ∫ v du

✏ Why These Matter

These properties and techniques allow us to evaluate complex integrals, model physical problems, and compute exact areas and quantities.
